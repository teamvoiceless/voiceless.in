About Us

Voiceless.in is an animal welfare initiative dedicated to helping injured and sick animals receive medical treatment. We believe every animal deserves care, compassion, and a chance to recover. Through community support and volunteers, we aim to make a meaningful difference in the lives of animals who cannot ask for help.

What we do and don't do: We arrange veterinary treatment and recovery support. We do not run an animal shelter and we do not offer adoption.

How We Help Animals

Our work is about getting an animal to the right treatment, and keeping people informed along the way.

🔍

Identifying animals in need

We look out for injured or sick animals and act on the cases brought to us.

🩺

Veterinary consultations

We coordinate consultations and treatment with veterinarians.

💚

Medical expense support

Where possible, we help cover the cost of an animal's treatment.

📝

Genuine treatment updates

We share honest updates on how each animal's treatment is going.

📣

Report an animal

See an animal that needs help? Email or message us on Instagram so we can look into it.

🤝

A volunteer community

We are building a community of kind people who want to help animals recover.
